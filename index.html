How it counts. Transaction fee and payment processing are charged on the item total plus shipping, after the discount. Offsite Ads (15%, or 12% for larger shops) is capped at 100 per order. The listing fee is 0.20 for each item sold when the listing auto-renews. Sales tax and VAT that Etsy collects are left out.
